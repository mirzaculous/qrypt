What is Qrypt

Ticketing built for
Pakistan's reality.

Pakistan's event industry loses millions every year to duplicate tickets, screenshots, and counterfeits. The current systems — static QR codes, printed passes, clipboard guest lists — were never built for how we actually use our phones.

Qrypt is a web-based smart ticketing platform that makes every ticket unique to the buyer, tied to their location, and impossible to forward, duplicate, or fake. No expensive hardware. No app download. Just a phone browser.

We're starting with concerts, university fests, corporate events, and functions across Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad — and we're charging a fraction of what existing platforms take.

How it works

Four steps.
Zero fraud.

01
🎟️
Organiser creates event

Set up your event in minutes. Define capacity, set a geofence boundary around your venue, and generate your unique event link. No hardware required.

02
📲
Attendee buys ticket

Buyer pays online and receives a personal ticket link. No app download. The ticket lives in their phone browser, tied cryptographically to their account.

03
📍
Location gets verified

When the attendee opens their ticket at the venue, Qrypt checks their GPS location against your geofence. Inside boundary? The live QR activates.

04
🔐
QR rotates every 3 seconds

The QR code changes cryptographically every 3 seconds. Scanner at the gate reads it instantly. Screenshot it, forward it, fake it — all invalid. Entry granted. Fraud blocked.

Features

Built different.
Not just different looking.

🔄
Rotating Cryptographic QR

Powered by TOTP-grade cryptography — the same algorithm behind Google Authenticator. Every code is mathematically unique, time-bound, and tied to a single person's device. Screenshot it. It's already expired.

📍
Geofenced Activation

Ticket only goes live when the attendee is physically at your venue. GPS + Wi-Fi fingerprinting for maximum accuracy indoors.

📊
Live Entry Dashboard

Watch who's entering in real time. Capacity alerts, entry heatmaps, and a full post-event attendance report.

📱
No App. No Hardware.

Attendees use their phone browser. Your gate staff uses any phone camera to scan. Zero setup costs.

⚡
Multiple Gate Scanners

Run 10 entry points simultaneously from different phones. All synced in real time. No more queues, no more chaos at the gate. Each scan is validated centrally in milliseconds — works even with intermittent venue Wi-Fi.
